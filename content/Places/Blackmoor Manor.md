**Location: Varrock, Waking World**

Blackmoor Manor serves as both the private residence and seat of power for [[Lord Marcus Blackmoor]], the ruling lord of [[Varrock]]. The estate stands as a testament to wealth and influence, though its grandeur masks darker secrets beneath its polished facade.

## Architecture and Grounds

The manor is an imposing structure of dark stone and timber, its architecture reflecting both defensive practicality and aristocratic comfort. Expansive grounds surround the main building, including:

• **The Main House:** A three-story structure with extensive private quarters, formal reception rooms, and Lord Blackmoor's study where he conducts official business.

• **The Moonlight Garden:** A carefully maintained garden featuring rare moonbell mushrooms and other nocturnal plants, though recently fallen into disrepair due to the household's distraction.

• **The Servant Quarters:** Well-appointed accommodations for the manor's staff, reflecting Lord Blackmoor's reputation as a fair employer.

• **The Stables:** Managed by [[Gareth and Lily]], housing fine horses and serving as an informal gathering place for estate workers.

• **The Chapel:** A small but beautifully appointed family chapel where Lord Blackmoor and his household attend private services, featuring stained glass windows and a peaceful atmosphere that offers respite from the manor's current troubles.

## Current Atmosphere

Once a place of quiet dignity and order, the manor now reflects the crisis gripping the household:

• **Overworked Staff:** The household runs on skeleton crews as Lord Blackmoor has dismissed many servants, claiming financial concerns.

• **Heightened Security:** Unusual numbers of guards patrol the grounds, and unfamiliar faces have been spotted watching the estate.

• **Palpable Tension:** The staff move with nervous energy, jumping at shadows and speaking in hushed tones about their young master's condition.

• **Oppressive Air:** Visitors often comment on feeling watched or unwelcome, as if the house itself harbors dark secrets.

## Key Household Members

• **[[Brennan]]:** The head steward, fiercely loyal but increasingly strained by his lord's erratic behavior and the household's mounting pressures.

• **[[Marta]]:** The cook, who maintains her kitchen as a refuge of normalcy while desperately seeking ways to help the afflicted boy.

• **[[Gareth and Lily]]:** The stable master and his daughter, who serve as informal eyes and ears of the estate's daily operations.

• **[[Willem]]:** The groundskeeper's assistant, struggling with unpaid wages while his pregnant wife needs care.

• **[[Elena]]:** The nursemaid who tends to Lord Blackmoor's son, heartbroken by her inability to wake the child from his supernatural slumber.

## The Shadow of Crisis

The manor's current state reflects the supernatural threat targeting the Blackmoor family. What was once a symbol of prosperity and stability has become a fortress under siege—not by armies, but by guilt, desperation, and forces from Lord Blackmoor's troubled past.

The very walls seem to hold secrets, and those who visit often sense that the true danger lurks not in the shadows outside, but in the weight of past sins that have finally come home to demand their price.
