**Type:** Minor Arc (4-6 sessions)

**Primary Goal:** Build loyalty to The Restless while subtly hinting at larger conspiracy

**Target Character:** Ronan (personal backstory integration)

## **Hook**
Wealthy citizens across multiple cities are losing precious memories - childhood moments, family gatherings, first loves. These stolen memories are being sold as "experience gems" on the black market, allowing buyers to live someone else's cherished moments.

## **Structure**

### **Act 1: The Investigation**
- Heroes track joint Nightshade Cartel/Slumber Exchange operation
- Discover targeting of "emotionally rich" memories
- Follow leads across multiple cities, interview victims

### **Act 2: The Personal Connection** 
- Uncover 20+ year history of the operation
- Find old records of "secondary targets" - information brokers and families
- **Revelation**: Ronan's father listed as someone who "knew about dock operations"
- **Truth**: Father died trying to expose memory theft ring targeting dock families, not as Exchange informant

### **Act 3: The Confrontation**
- Heroes confront Cartel/Exchange partnership
- Dreamscape battle with mysterious sand phenomena assistance
- The Restless arrives with perfect timing and unusual knowledge

## **Key Elements**
- **Emotional payoff**: Ronan learns father died as hero
- **Loyalty building**: Restless shows personal care and mysterious insight
- **Subtle hints**: Sand appearances, Restless's knowledge, "dreamscape remembers justice"
- **World building**: Cartel/Exchange cooperation, memory magic mechanics

## **Long-term Setup**
- Establishes Restless as wise, caring leader
- Plants seeds of Sandman involvement through sand phenomena
- Creates emotional investment that makes future betrayal devastating

## **Notes**
- Keep family connection reveal as genuine surprise (mid-arc)
- Focus on gradual revelation rather than obvious setup
- Balance personal stakes with larger guild mission
