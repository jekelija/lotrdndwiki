# Fangorn Forest — Campaign Sourcebook

---

## Read-Aloud Introduction: Entering Fangorn

_As you cross beneath the first leaning boughs of Fangorn, the world seems to exhale around you.  
The canopy closes overhead, dimming the light to a deep green twilight.  
The air is thick with the smell of ancient moss and damp earth.  
Every tree trunk seems older than memory—gnarled, towering, watchful._

_For a long moment, nothing moves.  
Then, somewhere deeper in the forest, you hear a low groan—  
like wood bending under a weight too old to bear.  
The sound is not quite wind… not quite voice…  
but it feels as though the forest has noticed you._

_And it does not feel like welcome._

## 1. Overview of Fangorn in the Fourth Age

Fangorn Forest, the eldest of the great woods of Middle-earth, has grown stranger and more powerful in the centuries after the fall of Sauron. Though the Ents still exist, their numbers continue to dwindle, and Treebeard himself is nearly impossible to find.

Yet Fangorn is no longer standing still.  
It is **advancing**.

The forest spreads outward—slowly, relentlessly—overrunning farmland, roads, guard posts, and isolated villages. Where once it was fading or dormant, it now grows with unsettling purpose.

Communities overtaken by the forest suffer eerie transformations:  
people vanish, and those who remain are forever changed.

---

## 2. The New Evil in Fangorn

The threat within Fangorn is older than Saruman and deeper-rooted than Sauron’s shadow. It was once imprisoned by the earliest Ents and long forgotten by the world of Men.

### 2A. The Root-Wyrm (Primordial Earth Spirit)

- A colossal, serpentine force of living wood, rot, and stone.
- Its stirring drives Fangorn’s unnatural expansion.
- Its hunger expresses itself through the forest’s silent advance.

### 2B. A Morgothic Seed (A Morgul-Tree)

- A parasitic creation of Morgoth, designed to drain will, memory, and life.
- Dormant for millennia, now awakened by shifting powers in the world.
- Its roots swallow identity and twist the minds of nearby living things.

### 2C. The Shade of Saruman

Not Saruman himself, but:

- A faint echo or psychic “imprint” left on corrupted Huorns.
- A whispering malice that accelerates the spread of Fangorn.
- Draws lost minds toward its influence.

---

## 3. Symptoms of Fangorn’s Expansion

### 3A. Physical Transformation

- Forest line advances yards each season.
- Roads buckle under sudden, twisting roots.
- Homes become wrapped in living bark overnight.
- Entire watchtowers vanish beneath rising foliage.

### 3B. Missing Persons

Those living near Fangorn’s edge vanish without trace:

- Doors left open
- Meals abandoned mid-bite
- Horses found wandering without riders
- Entire cottages empty, with only faint root-bursts in the floorboards

### 3C. The Changed Ones

Some people remain behind after the forest overtakes their home—  
but they are not the same.

They exhibit:

- Blank, unresponsive stares
- A slow, dreamlike gait
- Little concern for food or shelter
- A total lack of memory of friends or family
- Occasional whispers in broken, root-choked proto-Entish

Skin along the jaw or forearms may show branching, bark-like patterns—pale or dark, as though something beneath is trying to grow outward.

---

## 4. The Entwives’ Fate

The centuries-old mystery of the Entwives’ disappearance is now part of Fangorn’s central secret.

### 4A. Enslaved by the New Evil

- Their deep lore of cultivation was warped for its spread.
- They maintain enormous root networks against their will.
- Their power slows the creature, but also binds them in suffering.

### 4B. Guardians Containing the Darkness

- They found the threat ages ago and chose exile to contain it.
- Their underground gardens serve as a living magical seal.
- Their strength is failing, allowing Fangorn to surge outward.

Either path leads the party to the truth hidden beneath the forest.

---

## 5. Signs of Corruption and Unnatural Growth

- Trees shift position between nights.
- Gigantic roots burst through earth like coiled serpents.
- Entire groves align in spirals or radiating star-patterns.
- Pervasive, breathless silence with no wildlife sounds.
- Oily, black sap seeping from ancient trunks.
- Faded voices whispering in urgent, frightened Entish.

---

## 6. Adventure Elements Caused by the Expansion

### Hook 1 — “A Town Consumed”

Half a village swallowed by the encroaching woods.  
Those left alive wander aimlessly, empty-eyed.

### Hook 2 — “The Riders Who Never Returned”

Rohan patrols vanish on the edges of Fangorn; only horses come back.

### Hook 3 — “The Hollow Men”

Affected villagers wander at night toward the forest’s heart, as if summoned.

### Hook 4 — “The Entwife’s Cry”

A withered Entwife collapses at a settlement border, her whispered warning:

> “We cannot hold it… much longer…”

---

## 7. The Entwives’ Underground Gardens

Beneath Fangorn lies a vast, cultivated labyrinth of:

- Stone-latticed roots
- Bioluminescent flowers
- Ancient songs woven through living bark
- The ancient seal binding the primordial evil

Depending on your chosen narrative:

- The Entwives are imprisoned
- The Entwives are guardians
- The seal is failing
- The underground gardens are partially corrupted

---

## 8. Subtle Clue Connecting Fangorn to Minas Tirith

A nearly-eroded marble fragment lies within a corrupted clearing.  
It bears a partial inscription:

> “...stone-city’s silent guard...”

The stone is unmistakably:

- Gondorian marble
- Of a variety used only in the Court of the Fountain or around the White Tree
- Brought here long ago for a purpose now forgotten

This clue means nothing alone.  
Only after other hints are found does Minas Tirith become significant.

---

## 9. Key Locations in the Fangorn Arc

### The Root-Chasm

A vast, ancient cavern of writhing roots where the creature sleeps—or dreams.

### The Hidden Groves of the Entwives

A network of sacred gardens beneath the forest, half sanctuary, half prison.

### Overtaken Settlements

Towns nestled against Fangorn’s eaves are now silent, half-swallowed by trees.

### The Lost Garden of Yavanna

A once-holy glade whose fate is uncertain—corrupted, consumed, or defended.

---

## 10. Endgame Outcomes

### 1. Free the Entwives

They are liberated, but the ancient evil may awaken fully.

### 2. Restore or Reinforce the Seal

The creature is bound again, but the Entwives’ freedom or lives may be sacrificed.

### 3. Destroy the Evil

Requires rare alliances, ancient lore, or artifacts of immense power.

### 4. The Forest Marches

The seal fails. Fangorn becomes an expanding, unstoppable world threat.
