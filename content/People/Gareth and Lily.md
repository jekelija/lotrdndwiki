## **Gareth - Stable Master**

**Age:** Early 40s

**Appearance:** A weathered man with calloused hands and the practical build of someone who works with horses daily. His clothes are sturdy and often carry the scent of leather and hay.

### **Personality**

Gareth is straightforward and protective, especially when it comes to his daughter. Years of managing horses have given him a good sense for when something doesn't feel right.

**Demeanor:** Gruff but good-hearted, fiercely protective of family
**Speech Pattern:** Plain-spoken, uses horse-related analogies
**Mannerisms:** Checks on horses when nervous, speaks more gently when discussing Lily

### **Current Situation**

Recently concerned about some troublemakers who have been bothering Lily. As a protective father, he's been more watchful of who comes and goes around the estate.

**Quote:** "Been working with horses long enough to know when something's not right. Something's got everyone on edge around here."

---

## **Lily - Scullery Maid**

**Age:** 19

**Appearance:** A young woman with her father's practical nature but a gentler demeanor. Often has soap-stained hands from kitchen work and keeps her brown hair tied back with simple ribbons.

### **Personality**

Lily is kind-hearted and observant, moving through the household quietly but noticing everything. She's frightened by recent events but tries to stay strong for her father's sake.

**Demeanor:** Quiet but perceptive, easily startled lately
**Speech Pattern:** Soft-spoken, tends to whisper when sharing information
**Mannerisms:** Glances around nervously, fidgets with her apron strings

### **Knowledge and Access**

- Works throughout the house, sees comings and goings
- Knows servant gossip and household routines  
- Has access to back stairs and service areas
- Trusted by other young servants who confide in her

## **Father-Daughter Dynamic**

Gareth raised Lily alone after his wife died in childbirth. He's taught her to be independent but struggles with letting her face the world's dangers. Lily respects her father but sometimes wishes he'd trust her strength more.

**Shared Knowledge:**
- Both are concerned about recent tensions around the estate
- Both care deeply about the household's wellbeing
- Both have noticed everyone seems more worried lately

**Quote (Lily):** "The other girls are scared. Everyone's been on edge since the young master got sick. We just try to do our work and not make things worse."

## **Resources and Connections**

**Gareth's Domain:** Stables, carriage house, knowledge of who comes and goes
**Lily's Network:** Other young servants, access to most household areas
**Combined:** Back entrances, timing of guard rotations, safe places to meet privately
