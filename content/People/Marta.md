**Title:** Head Cook of the Blackmoor Estate

**Age:** Mid 50s

**Appearance:** A robust woman with flour-dusted apron and strong hands marked by years of kitchen work. Her graying hair is pulled back in a practical bun, and her face shows both kindness and determination.

## **Personality**

Marta is warm-hearted and practical, driven by a nurturing instinct to feed and care for others. The boy's refusal to eat has become a personal challenge that torments her daily.

**Demeanor:** Motherly but frustrated, eager to help
**Speech Pattern:** Direct and emotional, uses food metaphors
**Mannerisms:** Wipes hands on apron when thinking, tastes everything multiple times

## **Role and Responsibilities**

Marta has run the Blackmoor kitchen for fifteen years, taking pride in meals that please the family. She's known for her special broths and healing foods, passed down through generations of her family.

**Current Crisis:** Nothing she prepares can tempt the boy to eat more than a few bites. She's becoming desperate to find ingredients that might help, even pursuing expensive or rare items.

## **What Players Learn Initially**

- The boy barely eats anything anymore
- She's been trying different foods and remedies to help
- Needs special ingredients for healing broths
- Has been with the household for fifteen years

**Practical Skills:**
- Skilled cook with knowledge of healing foods
- Access to kitchen areas of the house
- Connections with local food merchants

**Quote:** "A child who won't eat is a child who's given up hope. There must be something - some flavor, some comfort - that can reach him."

## **Resources and Connections**

**The Kitchen:** Heart of servant activity, source of gossip and information
**Market Contacts:** Knows merchants who deal in rare ingredients
**Servant Network:** Other staff often gather in kitchen, sharing news and concerns

## **Relationships**

**To the Boy:** Protective, almost grandmotherly
**To Lord Blackmoor:** Respectful but worried about his neglect of proper meals
**To Other Staff:** Central figure, offers food and comfort to fellow servants
