**Title:** Head Steward of the Blackmoor Estate

**Age:** Late 40s

**Appearance:** A tall, thin man with graying hair kept meticulously neat. Always impeccably dressed in formal steward attire, with a bearing that speaks of decades of service to nobility.

## **Personality**

Brennan is the embodiment of professional discretion and proper protocol. He speaks in measured tones and maintains formal distance even when being helpful. Years of managing a noble household have made him an excellent judge of character and intention.

**Demeanor:** Polite but firm, protective of his lord's privacy
**Speech Pattern:** Formal, using titles and proper address
**Mannerisms:** Adjusts his collar when stressed, stands with perfect posture

## **Role and Responsibilities**

As head steward, Brennan oversees all household operations, manages the other servants, and controls access to Lord Blackmoor. He has served the Blackmoor family for over twenty years and takes pride in maintaining the household's reputation.

**Current Stress:** The lord's distraction and the boy's condition have disrupted normal routines. Brennan struggles to maintain order while multiple "specialists" come and go, all failing to help.

## **What Players Learn Initially**

- Has served the Blackmoor family for over twenty years
- Multiple specialists have already attempted to help the boy
- Lord Blackmoor has become increasingly distracted and desperate
- Maintains strict protocols about who can see the lord

**Quote:** "His lordship has suffered enough disappointment from well-meaning strangers. If you wish an audience, you must prove yourselves worthy of his time."

## **Relationships**

**To Lord Blackmoor:** Loyal, protective, genuinely concerned for both father and son
**To Other Staff:** Respected authority figure, maintains professional relationships
**To Visitors:** Cautious but not unkind, willing to help those who prove themselves sincere
