**Title:** Groundskeeper's Assistant

**Age:** Late 20s

**Appearance:** A lean young man with dirt under his fingernails and sun-weathered skin from outdoor work. His clothes are patched but clean, and he has an anxious energy about him lately.

## **Personality**

Willem is hardworking but currently desperate due to unpaid wages and his wife's pregnancy. Normally honest and reliable, his current circumstances make him vulnerable to questionable decisions.

**Demeanor:** Nervous, glances around frequently, eager to please
**Speech Pattern:** Speaks quickly when anxious, often mentions his wife
**Mannerisms:** Rubs the back of his neck when lying, counts coins obsessively

## **Current Situation**

Willem hasn't been paid in three months due to Lord Blackmoor's distraction. His pregnant wife needs medical care they can't afford, making him desperate for any source of income.

**Personal Crisis:**
- Wife is six months pregnant with complications
- Owes rent on their small cottage
- Master groundskeeper has been ill, leaving Willem to handle everything alone
- Considering abandoning his position to find work elsewhere

## **Knowledge and Information**

Willem works the night shift often and has noticed things others miss:

**What He Might Share:**
- Works mostly night shifts maintaining the grounds
- The moonlight garden has had problems with pests lately
- Knows the layout of the estate grounds well
- Has been handling extra work since the head groundskeeper fell ill

**Practical Knowledge:**
- Layout of garden areas and outdoor spaces
- Which areas need maintenance work
- Location of gardening tools and supplies
- Understanding of the estate's grounds

## **Moral Conflict**

Willem knows information that might be valuable, but he's torn between:
- Loyalty to the Blackmoor family who employed him
- Desperate need for money to care for his wife
- Fear of the strange things he's witnessed
- Worry about getting involved in something dangerous

**Quote:** "I've got a baby coming and bills to pay. Times are tough when the wages don't come regular, but we all just hope things get better soon."

## **Resources and Access**

- Keys to garden sheds and outdoor storage
- Knowledge of which areas are private vs. accessible
- Access to groundskeeper uniforms and tools
- Understanding of estate's outdoor security

## **Relationships**

**To His Wife:** Devoted, would do anything to protect her
**To the Groundskeeper:** Loyal subordinate, worried about his mentor's illness  
**To Other Staff:** Friendly but keeps to himself lately due to shame about unpaid wages
