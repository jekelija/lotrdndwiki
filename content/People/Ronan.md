**Race:** Human

**Class:** Ranger

**Background:** Bounty Hunter

**Affiliation:** [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]

**Status:** Active Guild Operative

Ronan is a human ranger and bounty hunter whose harsh upbringing shaped him into a formidable tracker and survivor. His journey from orphaned street urchin to guild operative reflects both his exceptional skills and his ongoing quest for justice regarding his family's mysterious death.

**Background and Early Life**

Ronan was born in the bustling port city of Varrock, where the divide between wealthy merchants and struggling poor was as wide as the ocean itself. His parents, laborers at the docks, died in a warehouse fire when he was only six years old. While authorities claimed it was an accident, whispers on the street spoke of a nobleman's sabotage to drive out competition, leaving Ronan orphaned and penniless in the city's underbelly.

As a street urchin, Ronan survived by wit and agility, scavenging food and evading guards. He learned to navigate shadows, eavesdrop on the powerful, and pick locks to access places where scraps of wealth could be stolen. Life on the streets hardened him, teaching him to distrust anyone who claimed to help, as he learned that kindness often came with strings attached.

**Mentorship and Training**

When Ronan was twelve, he encountered Gideon, a retired ranger wandering the city's outskirts. Gideon saw potential in the scrappy boy and took him under his wing, teaching him to hunt, track, and survive in the wilderness—skills that far surpassed the narrow alleyways of his youth. Though Gideon tried to instill lessons of justice, balance, and loyalty, these teachings didn't fully take root in a boy who had learned the world was a dark place where survival came first.

**Life as a Bounty Hunter**

As a bounty hunter, Ronan seeks both survival and a twisted sense of justice. His targets are often criminals, debtors, or runaway servants, but he doesn't discriminate—he'll take anyone if the price is right. Despite his seemingly cold motivations, Ronan maintains an unspoken code: he never harms children and despises slavers or those who exploit the vulnerable.

**The Quest for Truth**

Beneath his hardened exterior, Ronan remains haunted by the mysterious fire that claimed his parents. He has spent years investigating, piecing together fragments of information about the nobleman rumored to have orchestrated their deaths. Every bounty he hunts brings him closer to uncovering the truth, as he uses his growing network of informants to track down answers. For Ronan, the bounty board represents more than coin—it's a means to an end in his quest for closure.

**Guild Service**

Ronan's exceptional performance during his [[Session 01 - The Kindleglow Test|entrance examination]] demonstrated rare natural talent for [[Dreamwalking]]. He was one of only three candidates to achieve lucidity during the test—an extraordinarily uncommon occurrence that immediately marked him as a gifted dreamwalker.

His ranger and bounty hunting skills, honed through years of tracking quarry and navigating dangerous situations, translate remarkably well to the fluid and unpredictable environments of the dreamscape. His ability to read environmental signs, adapt to changing circumstances, and remain calm under pressure proved invaluable when the entrance test took an unexpected and dangerous turn with the manifestation of [[The Lost Man]].

The guild's mission aligns with Ronan's personal code of protecting the vulnerable, though his membership also serves his broader goal of expanding his network and resources in his ongoing investigation into his parents' death.

**Companions**

Ronan adventures alongside [[Aargon]] and [[Ryker]], forming a exceptional team that achieved complete team lucidity during their guild entrance examination. This rare feat, combined with their survival of an encounter with [[The Lost Man]], has established them as potentially legendary figures within the guild's history.

The trio's complementary skills and proven ability to work together under extreme circumstances makes them a formidable unit for the guild's most challenging operations in the dreamscape.
