**Class:** Ranger

**Affiliation:** [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]

**Status:** Active Guild Operative

Ronan is a ranger who became one of [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]'s most promising recruits after an extraordinary entrance examination that defied all expectations. His natural connection to the wilderness and keen tracking abilities have proven valuable assets in both the waking world and the dreamscape.

**Guild Service**

Ronan's exceptional performance during his [[Session 01 - The Kindleglow Test|entrance examination]] demonstrated rare natural talent for [[Dreamwalking]]. He was one of only three candidates to achieve lucidity during the test—an extraordinarily uncommon occurrence that immediately marked him as a gifted dreamwalker.

His ranger skills, honed through years of wilderness experience, translate surprisingly well to navigating the fluid and unpredictable environments of the dreamscape. His ability to track quarry, read environmental signs, and adapt to changing circumstances proved invaluable when the entrance test took an unexpected and dangerous turn with the manifestation of [[The Lost Man]].

**Companions**

Ronan adventures alongside [[Aargon]] and [[Ryker]], forming a exceptional team that achieved complete team lucidity during their guild entrance examination. This rare feat, combined with their survival of an encounter with [[The Lost Man]], has established them as potentially legendary figures within the guild's history.

The trio's complementary skills and proven ability to work together under extreme circumstances makes them a formidable unit for the guild's most challenging operations in the dreamscape.
