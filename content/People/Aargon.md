**Race:** Half-Elf

**Class:** Paladin

**Affiliation:** [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]

**Status:** Active Guild Operative

Aargon is a half-elf paladin driven by a deep personal mission to combat the dark and sinister powers that plague the world. His journey toward this purpose began with a profound loss and subsequent liberation that shaped his worldview and dedication to protecting others from similar fates.

**Background and Motivation**

Aargon's brother fell victim to a necromancer's dark magic, suffering under undead corruption until Aargon could organize a rescue mission. Working alongside a human wizard and an orc fighter, they successfully avenged his brother by confronting and defeating the necromancer responsible. 

After this victory and the liberation of his brother from undead misery, Aargon struggled to find new purpose. The traumatic experience of witnessing his brother's suffering left him questioning his role in the world. Through reflection and prayer, he realized that there was a need to rid the world of all dark and sinister powers.

Aargon now sees himself as a potential beacon of hope—a powerful light in lands that have grown dark from the despair of hopelessness. His mission extends beyond personal vengeance or glory; he seeks to prevent others from experiencing the kind of loss and corruption that claimed his brother.

**Philosophy and Goals**

After parting ways with the human wizard and orc fighter following their successful mission, Aargon set out to gather a new team that would aid him in tackling the fiercest foes. This collaborative approach reflects his understanding that the greatest evils require united strength to defeat. 

He found his new companions in [[Ronan]] and [[Ryker]], forming a bond through shared adventures before their eventual recruitment by [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]. His recruitment by the guild aligns perfectly with his vision, providing him with both the team he sought and a mission worthy of his dedication.

**Guild Service**

Aargon's exceptional performance during his [[Session 01 - The Kindleglow Test|entrance examination]] demonstrated not only his natural aptitude for [[Dreamwalking]] but also his ability to maintain focus and purpose even when facing unprecedented dangers like [[The Lost Man]]. His achievement of lucidity during the test, alongside his companions, marked him as one of the guild's most promising new recruits.

His paladin training and divine connection may provide unique advantages in combating the malevolent forces that threaten the dreamscape, making him a valuable asset in the guild's ongoing mission to protect dreamers from harm.

**Companions**

Aargon adventures alongside [[Ronan]] and [[Ryker]], forming a formidable team that achieved the rare feat of complete team lucidity during their guild entrance examination. Together, they represent a new generation of guild operatives with exceptional potential for dreamwalking operations.
