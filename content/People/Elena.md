**Title:** Nursemaid to the Blackmoor Household

**Age:** Early 50s

**Appearance:** A gentle woman with graying hair and kind eyes that show the strain of recent worry. Her clothes are simple but well-maintained, and she moves with the quiet efficiency of someone accustomed to caring for others.

## **Personality**

Elena is compassionate and devoted to the children in her care. She has a nurturing nature but has become increasingly distressed by her inability to help the young master.

**Demeanor:** Caring but worried, easily moved to emotion when discussing the boy
**Speech Pattern:** Soft-spoken, uses endearing terms for children
**Mannerisms:** Wrings her hands when distressed, always checking on others' comfort

## **Role and Responsibilities**

Elena has cared for the Blackmoor children for many years, serving as both nursemaid and a motherly figure in the household. She takes personal responsibility for the children's wellbeing and health.

**Current Distress:** The young master's condition has deeply affected her. She feels helpless watching him waste away despite all her care and attention.

## **What Players Learn Initially**

- Has been with the family for many years
- Deeply cares for the boy's wellbeing  
- Has tried various remedies and comfort measures
- Is willing to help anyone who might genuinely be able to assist
- Knows the boy's daily routines and habits

**Quote:** "That sweet child just lies there, barely eating, barely responding. I've tried everything I know, but nothing reaches him anymore. If you truly think you can help..."

## **Resources and Access**

- Intimate knowledge of the boy's daily care needs
- Access to the family's living areas
- Trusted by the household staff
- Understanding of what treatments have already been attempted

## **Relationships**

**To the Boy:** Protective, almost maternal love
**To Lord Blackmoor:** Respectful but concerned about his neglect of self-care
**To Other Staff:** Trusted figure, often confided in by other servants
