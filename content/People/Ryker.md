**Full Name:** Ryker Briarstone

**Race:** Human

**Class:** Druid

**Alignment:** Neutral Good

**Affiliation:** [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]

**Status:** Active Guild Operative

Ryker Briarstone is a human druid whose tragic past and deep connection to the natural world have shaped him into both a protector of the wilds and a healer of lands scarred by exploitation. His recruitment into [[The Dreamweavers Guild]] came after an entrance examination that shattered all precedents and established him as one of the guild's most gifted new operatives.

**Early Life and Tragedy**

Born in a small village on the edge of the Cloakwood Forest, Ryker spent his childhood surrounded by the wonders of the natural world. His mother, a skilled herbalist, and his father, a former ranger, taught him to respect the delicate balance of life. Ryker often wandered the forest, forming bonds with its creatures and listening to the ancient whispers of the trees.

When Ryker was twelve, his world was shattered. A mercenary band, desperate for resources, pillaged his village and set the forest ablaze. Both his parents perished in the flames, leaving Ryker with nothing but his grief and a small wooden pendant carved by his father. Lost and alone, he fled deep into the forest until he collapsed from exhaustion.

**The Circle of Whispering Pines**

Ryker was found by the Circle of Whispering Pines, a secluded order of druids dedicated to preserving the harmony of nature. Led by a wise elf named Lorin, who saw a kindred spirit in the boy, the Circle took him in and cared for him. Under Lorin's guidance, Ryker learned to harness the natural magic of the world and developed a deep understanding of the cycles of life and death.

Years of training with the Circle shaped Ryker into a skilled druid and protector of the wilds. Yet, as he grew, so did his dreams of the wider world. Visions of a towering oak with golden leaves began to fill his mind—a symbol of unity between nature and civilization. The forest seemed to urge him onward, and Ryker realized his destiny lay beyond the Whispering Pines.

**Goals and Philosophy**

Ryker seeks to heal lands devastated by exploitation and foster coexistence between people and nature. While his personal tragedy drives him, he is not consumed by vengeance; instead, he believes in education and restoration, striving to show others the beauty and power of the natural world. With his heart full of purpose, he set out to heal the scars left by war and greed, determined to prevent others from experiencing the loss he endured.

**Personality and Bonds**

Ryker approaches challenges with patience and a steady demeanor, often acting as a mediator in tense situations. He is protective and loyal, going to great lengths to shield his companions, the wilderness, and innocent lives from harm. He keeps his father's wooden pendant as a cherished memento of his lost family, while the Circle of Whispering Pines remains his spiritual family—he considers himself their ambassador in the wider world.

Despite his noble goals, Ryker struggles to forgive those who waste animal life or disturb nature's balance through actions like poisoning waterways, culling species, or setting forests ablaze. Additionally, cities and large crowds make him uneasy, as he feels disconnected from the natural rhythms he's attuned to.

**Guild Service**

Ryker's exceptional performance during his [[Session 01 - The Kindleglow Test|entrance examination]] revealed natural talents that extend far beyond typical druidic abilities. He achieved lucidity during the test—a rare accomplishment made even more extraordinary by the fact that his entire team accomplished the same feat simultaneously.

His druidic understanding of natural cycles, transformation, and the interconnectedness of all things translates remarkably well to the dreamscape's fluid and symbolic environment. Where others struggle with the dream realm's constantly shifting nature, Ryker's familiarity with natural metamorphosis allows him to adapt and flow with the changes.

When the entrance test took a dangerous turn with the unexpected manifestation of [[The Lost Man]], Ryker's calm demeanor and natural resilience helped maintain team cohesion during an encounter that could have easily proven fatal for less experienced dreamwalkers. His role in the guild aligns with his broader mission to protect and heal, extending his guardianship from the natural world to the realm of dreams.

**Companions**

Ryker adventures alongside [[Aargon]] and [[Ronan]], forming a remarkably gifted team that achieved complete team lucidity during their guild entrance examination. Their survival of an encounter with [[The Lost Man]]—an entity most guild members have never seen—has marked them as exceptional recruits with potentially legendary futures within the organization.

The trio's diverse backgrounds and complementary abilities, combined with their proven teamwork under extreme circumstances, position them as valuable assets for the guild's most critical dreamwalking operations.
