# The Purists

> _“The age of wonder was never ours to keep.”_

---

## Overview

**Creed:** _Destroy it; magic was never meant for Men._  
**Hidden Vice:** _The final disenchantment of the world; beauty sacrificed._

The Purists seek an end to magic itself. They believe that the fading of enchantment is the **will of Eru**, and that clinging to it only invites ruin.  
They see themselves as surgeons cutting away corruption — not villains, but liberators of a weary world.

---

## Philosophy

Magic, they argue, was a **gift to the Firstborn**, not to Men.  
The continued presence of such forces has only bred pride, war, and decay. Better a dull but peaceful world than a brilliant one forever in peril.

Their greatest flaw is their **certainty** — their willingness to burn beauty for safety.

---

- Loose. No true organization.
