# 📜 The State of Knowledge of the Third Age

Although in the preceding four centuries much of the detail surrounding the **One Ring** and the **War of the Ring** has faded into myth, some truths remain known among the peoples of Middle-earth. Most common folk today possess only a _broad understanding_ — the same grasp one might expect of a casual reader who once heard the tale long ago.

The average person remembers the _basic plot_: that a dark lord named **Sauron** sought dominion over the world through a ring of power; that a fellowship of heroes rose against him; and that through great courage and sacrifice, the Ring was unmade and peace restored.

Yet much else is lost or distorted. Names blur, timelines contradict, and the motives of ancient figures are debated in taverns as often as in halls of learning.

Each of your characters may know _more_ or _less_ of these histories depending on their upbringing and heritage, as reflected in your individual backstories. However, bear in mind that what you “know” may not be _known fact_ — even historians of the present age **disagree** on many points of the old tales.

## ⚔️ Known Controversies

### **The Death of Saruman**

It is **unknown how Saruman the White met his end**.

- Some claim he was **stabbed by his servant, Wormtongue**, after his humiliation in the Shire following the **Battle of Bywater**.
- Others insist that the **Battle of Bywater itself is legend**, and that Saruman was **cast from the heights of Orthanc** by his own creation or rival.

No account is universally accepted, and the truth — if it was ever written — has long since been lost to time.

## 🕰️ Timeline of the Fourth Age

### **Year 1 — The Dawn of the Fourth Age**

- The **One Ring is destroyed**, and **Sauron is unmade**.
- **Aragorn II Elessar** is crowned High King of the Reunited Kingdom of **Gondor and Arnor**.
- The **Havens of the Elves** begin to empty as the Eldar sail West.
- The **Age of Men** begins.

---

### **Year 120 — The Passing of the King**

- King **Elessar** dies after a reign of 122 years; **Queen Arwen** departs to Lothlórien and is never seen again.
- His son, **Eldarion Telcontar**, becomes King. The realm remains strong but less united.

---

### **Year 200–250 — The Fading of the Eldar**

- Most remaining Elves of Rivendell and Lindon depart across the sea.
- Trade between Men and Dwarves prospers.
- Rumors of Orcish resurgence in the Misty Mountains begin.

---

### **Year 300–350 — The Decline of the Reunited Kingdom**

- Civil unrest follows after the death of King Eldarion’s heir.
- Arnor’s northern territories fragment into independent lordships.
- Gondor maintains strength, but its borders shrink.
- Dwarves reclaim **Khazad-dûm (Moria)**, though deep shadows linger in its lower halls.

---

### **Year 370 — The Last Embassy of the Elves**

- A final Elven envoy visits Minas Tirith to deliver the last recorded message from Valinor.
- The message warns: _“Even without the Eye, the world will remember its shape.”_
- Within the decade, all known Elves of the West vanish from mortal lands.

---

### **Year 400 — The Present Day**

- The **Reunited Kingdom** is no more; its successor realms feud and compete.
- Orcish tribes gather beneath the mountains once more.
- Rumors speak of **strange relics** emerging from the ruins of Isengard, Mordor, and the Barrow-downs.
- Magic is fading, yet shadows stir — as if the land itself remembers the age of power.
