The tables below are a guideline of my thoughts. If you have a race or a class that you want to play and you think you have a good reason for it to exist in this setting, just shoot me a message!

Note if you pick a non-standard race/class, please let me know and we'll come up with a character sheet together along with different spells/modifiers you may have that are not part of D&D standard edition.

| **Class**                        | **Fit in Middle-earth**     | **Notes / Adaptation**                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fighter / Ranger / Barbarian** | ✅ Core archetypes          | Rangers are Dúnedain traditions; Fighters are men-at-arms. Barbarians could be wild Northmen.                                                            |
| **Rogue**                        | ✅ Fits perfectly           | Scouts, spies, burglars, wanderers — very Middle-earth.                                                                                                  |
| **Cleric / Paladin**             | ⚠️ Heavily re-flavor        | No “gods” or radiant spells. Channel faith through Valar reverence or ancestral oaths. Miracles are rare, subtle.                                        |
| **Wizard / Sorcerer / Warlock**  | ❌ Not playable             | True magic users are gone (Gandalf and the Istari). Even Elves now rely on memory, not power. Maybe one scholar who uses ancient lore for minor effects. |
| **Bard**                         | ✅ Excellent if grounded    | Bardic magic = song and lore, not spellcasting. Think of song awakening hearts, not casting “fireball.”                                                  |
| **Druid**                        | ⚠️ Rare and re-flavored     | Could represent an old “Green-speech” of the Entwives or the last keepers of Yavanna’s lore. No shapeshifting, no spell list.                            |
| **Monk**                         | ❌ Doesn’t fit              | No monastic combat traditions in Arda. Remove.                                                                                                           |
| **Artificer**                    | ⚠️ Possible in small doses  | If anyone is “tinkering,” they’re inheritors of Saruman’s or Dwarven craft — focus on devices, not magic items. Fits the industrial tension.             |
| **Skin-changer**                 | ⚠️ Possible in small doses, | Druid-like, must be race of Man https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Skin-changers                                                                               |

| **Race**                                                | **Fit in Middle-earth**     | **Notes / Adaptation**                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Men**                                                 | ✅ Central                  | The Dominion of Men; diverse cultures — think Rohirrim descendants, Gondorian nobility, Haradrim tribes, Easterling survivors.  |
| **Elves**                                               | ⚠️ Extremely rare           | Almost all have sailed West. One or two ancient holdouts could remain, fading, strange, and sorrowful. PCs? Maybe one, maximum. |
| **Dwarves**                                             | ✅ but dwindling            | Fewer than ever; most halls closed or hidden. Could play a wandering craftsman, the last of a fading line.                      |
| **Hobbits**                                             | ⚠️ Optional, isolationist   | Still in the Shire (closed to outsiders). A hobbit abroad would be a myth come alive. One PC at most.                           |
| **Half-elves**                                          | ❌ None left                | Arwen’s line was the last; they chose mortality.                                                                                |
| **Orcs / Goblins / Uruks**                              | ⚠️ Playable                 | Sauron’s domination gone; a few feral tribes survive. Could be an atonement or curiosity arc, but not a party norm.             |
| **Spider**                                              | ⚠️ Playable but convince me | Shelob's offspring                                                                                                              |
| **Ents / Entwives**                                     | ❌ Functionally gone        | No sign after Third Age. The wilderness may whisper of them, but they’re not characters.                                        |
| **Badger-folk**                                         | ⚠️ Rare                     | https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Badger-folk                                                                                        |
| **The Eagles**                                          | ⚠️ Rare                     | Similar to aarakocra in D&D https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Eagles                                                           |
| **Maiar or Ainur**                                      | ❌ Completely gone          | Their time in Arda is ended — only echoes remain.                                                                               |
| **Tom Bombardil**                                       | ❌ Ha, no.                  | Please don't.                                                                                                                   |
| **Other D&D races (tiefling, dragonborn, gnome, etc.)** | ❌ Almost Totally out       | Not part of Tolkien’s world. Introduce nothing planar or non-Arda. You could maybe convince me on dragon-born                   |
