title: "Session 3 – Seven Shadows Fall"
---

📍 Locations

Barrow Tomb

Derelict River Crossing

Displaced Human Village

Fangorn Forest

📜 Summary

After defeating the Barrow-Wraiths, the party explores the tomb and uncovers both treasure and a cryptic clue.

📜 Clue Discovered

“…for the crown may only rest where seven shadows fall.”

🎒 Loot Recovered

Helm of Understanding Languages

Scroll of Speak with Dead

Weather-Worn Ranger Half-Cloak

Signet Ring of Gondor

Leaving the tomb, the party attempts to cross a dangerous river via a crumbling bridge. The crossing goes poorly when Thalan falls into the rushing water, but he is saved just in time when Aerion flies out and pulls him to safety.

🏘️ The Displaced Village

Continuing onward, the party encounters a human village, where some inhabitants bear the same insignia as the bandits previously fought. Despite this, the villagers show no hostility.

The party speaks with the village leader and learns the settlement was displaced from the edges of Gondor due to the unnatural expansion of Fangorn Forest.

🌲 Fangorn’s Growth

The forest advances 10–20 feet per day

Expansion occurs only when unobserved

Fully grown trees appear suddenly

Once a settlement is enveloped:

People begin disappearing

Strange phenomena occur

Villages are forced to migrate west and north

🌶️ Flavor Moment

The party purchases peppers from a local chef before departing.

🐺 Encounter

Venturing into Fangorn Forest, the party fails to find a clear route to Isengard and instead encounters a Warg.

🔚 Session End

The session ends with the looming threat of Fangorn—and the Warg—before them.
