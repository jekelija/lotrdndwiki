Session 4 – The Tower of Silent Words
📍 Locations

Fangorn Forest (Edge)

Isengard – Outer Ring

Orthanc (The Tower of Isengard)

🎭 Notable NPCs

Istravar Dengrath
Thalan’s former commander; Ranger of the Fourth Age

📜 Summary

The party confronts the Warg encountered in Fangorn Forest and defeats it with relative ease.

🐺 Loot Recovered

Warg Pelt

Warg Poison

🏰 Isengard Revisited

The party enters the outer ring of Isengard, where they are unexpectedly confronted by Istravar Dengrath, Thalan’s former commander. He is accompanied by three fellow Rangers, all tasked with uncovering clues to the location of Saruman’s Crown within Isengard.

Istravar shares what is known of Isengard’s fate in the Fourth Age:

King Elessar restored the fortress after the War of the Ring

When the Stewards regained control of Gondor, Isengard fell once more into neglect

Many chambers of Orthanc were thoroughly searched

The means to access certain sealed rooms were lost to history

🤝 Alliance Formed

The two groups agree to cooperate in searching the tower:

Istravar’s party searches the even-numbered floors

The Order of the Crown searches the odd-numbered floors

🏯 Exploring Orthanc

Each floor of the tower follows a similar design:

One large circular central chamber

Three smaller side rooms branching from it

🧱 First Floor

Contains meeting and gathering rooms

One locked door is noted but not investigated

The party decides to continue upward

⚔️ Third Floor

Appears to be an armory

Nothing of use is recovered

🗝️ Fifth Floor

The party encounters a curious enchanted talking door, which agrees to open only if the group can entertain it with riddles.

🎒 Loot Recovered

Various minor items

Dark-Gold Rusted Key
Its purpose unknown

🔕 Seventh Floor – Saruman’s Study

At the top of the tower, the party discovers what is believed to be Saruman’s private study. Only a single door blocks their path.

🧩 The Silent Riddle

The door bears an inscription in Dark Speech, deciphered by Aerion using the Helm of Understanding Languages:

“Once spoken, I am broken.”

The party attempts multiple solutions, but each time any sound is made, the door responds by firing enchanted darts.

After several painful attempts, the party devises a clever solution:

They retrieve the talking door from the fifth floor to use as a shield

They realize the solution is not to speak the answer—but to make the room itself silent

In doing so, they chuck an explosive vial out the window and almost hit Istravar's party.

When all sound ceases, the door opens.

🕳️ Discovery

Inside the chamber is:

An empty circular pedestal

Metal clamps arranged as if to hold a circular object

The pedestal is bare—but unmistakably designed to hold something powerful.

🔚 Session End

The party stands before the empty pedestal, knowing they are close—but also realizing the crown has already been moved.
