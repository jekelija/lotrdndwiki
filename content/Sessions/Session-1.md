📜 Summary

The group meets in Bree at the Prancing Pony, where they formally establish themselves as The Order of the Crown. They are introduced to their patron, Tolman Gardner, who reveals his purpose in sponsoring them.

Tolman explains that scholars and archaeologists have uncovered evidence suggesting Saruman the White once crafted a Crown of Power—an artifact never recovered after his fall. Multiple factions are now rumored to be searching for it, and Tolman believes the party may be able to find it first.

To aid them, Tolman gifts Otbert a unique weapon:

🗡️ Magic Item

The Shard of Western Glow
A dagger forged from fragments of the famed blade Sting

🗺️ Journey Begins

The party sets out toward Isengard, hoping to uncover clues about the crown’s location. Along the road, they encounter a small Hobbit village under threat from human bandits.

⚔️ Combat Encounter

The party defeats the bandits and drives them away, securing the village.

🔚 Session End

The session concludes with the village saved and the party continuing their journey eastward.
