**Date:** First Session  
**Location:** Kindleglow Festival Town → [[Dreamweavers Guild Headquarters]]  
**Key Figures:** [[Derrin "Drift" Thistledown]], [[Rorik Fennhart]], [[The Lost Man]]

## The Heroes

**Aargon** - Paladin  
**Ronan** - Ranger  
**Ryker** - Druid

Three seasoned adventurers whose previous travels had caught the attention of [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]. Unknown to them, they were being evaluated for potential recruitment.

## The Kindleglow Festival

The heroes found themselves in a festive town celebrating [[Kindleglow (Holiday)]], where the seasonal spirit filled the air with warmth and community. It was during this celebration that they encountered [[Derrin "Drift" Thistledown]], a halfling who approached them with an unusual proposition.

## The Contract

Drift presented the heroes with a seemingly straightforward bounty: capture a bandit leader known as "the Grunch" who had stolen Kindleglow gifts and decorations from the town. The heroes, agreeing to the terms, retired at the local inn for the night before setting out on their mountain expedition.

## The Mountain Pursuit

The next morning, the heroes tracked the Grunch to his mountain hideout, where they engaged his goblin henchmen and a wolf companion named Max. During the battle, both the Grunch and Max escaped through a corridor deeper into the mountain.

Following their quarry, the heroes discovered the corridor led to what appeared to be a dead end—a frozen chamber with hanging icicles and a solid pond of ice. Through careful investigation, Aargon discovered that striking the icicles in a specific musical pattern (reminiscent of jingle bells) caused the frozen pond to melt.

## The Transition

One by one, the heroes leaped into the melted water, expecting to pursue their target. Instead, they found themselves pulled into an entirely different environment—no longer in a wintry mountain landscape, but in warm ocean waters near a pirate ship crewed by the Grunch and his remaining henchmen.

## The Awakening

As the surreal battle continued, strange phenomena began occurring that defied the heroes' understanding of reality. Gradually, each hero began achieving **lucidity**—the realization that they were dreaming. As this awareness dawned, they could hear [[Derrin "Drift" Thistledown]]'s voice in their minds, guiding them as a [[Sleep Talkers|Sleep Talker]], helping them navigate the dream's obstacles.

With lucidity came power—the ability to bend dream reality and perform feats impossible in the waking world.

## The Lost Dreamwalker's Intervention

During the climactic battle with the Grunch, massive pulse waves began rippling through the dreamscape. The waves started slowly but quickly intensified, creating devastating effects that knocked the heroes from their feet. The ocean drained away, the pirate ship crashed to the ground, and the Grunch vanished entirely.

[[The Lost Man]] manifested in the dream—not physically thrashing, but his very presence causing widespread destruction throughout the dreamscape. The heroes attempted to engage this new threat but quickly discovered their inadequacy against such overwhelming power. Even their enhanced lucid abilities proved insufficient against this entity.

The heroes could hear Drift's increasingly frantic voice in their minds as he communicated with others: "Get them out of there!" His panic was evident as he realized the true magnitude of what was occurring.

## The Revelation

The heroes were pulled from the dream and awakened to find themselves in [[Dreamweavers Guild Headquarters]]. [[Rorik Fennhart]] revealed the truth: the entire Grunch pursuit had been an elaborate entrance examination designed to test their worthiness for guild membership.

The heroes learned about [[The Dreamweavers Guild]]'s true purpose as guardians of the dreamscape, and were offered the opportunity to join their ranks. All three accepted, officially beginning their careers as dream operatives.

## Exceptional Circumstances

This entrance examination proved to be extraordinarily unusual and dangerous, far beyond what [[The Dreamweavers Guild]] had intended or experienced before:

**Unprecedented Team Lucidity:** It is exceptionally rare for an entire team to achieve lucidity during an entrance test. The fact that all three heroes—Aargon, Ronan, and Ryker—successfully became lucid suggests they possess natural gifts as dreamwalkers that far exceed typical recruits.

**The Lost Man's Manifestation:** The appearance of [[The Lost Man]] during the test was completely unexpected and unprecedented. Most guild members have never encountered this entity, and some had even begun to doubt his existence until this incident. His intervention transformed what should have been a controlled evaluation into a genuinely perilous situation.

**Unintended Danger:** The entrance exam was not designed to expose recruits to such extreme threats. [[The Lost Man]]'s presence disrupted the carefully planned scenario, creating chaos that even experienced guild operatives would find challenging to handle.

## Outcome

- Heroes successfully demonstrated exceptional potential for [[Dreamwalking]]
- All three heroes achieved lucidity during the test—an extremely rare occurrence
- Heroes survived an encounter with [[The Lost Man]], providing the guild with crucial confirmation of his existence
- The test revealed the heroes possess unusual natural abilities as dreamwalkers
- Formal recruitment into [[The Dreamweavers Guild]] completed under extraordinary circumstances
- Introduction to the guild's mission and purpose established, along with an understanding of the true dangers they face

---

*This session marked not only the beginning of the heroes' journey into the mysteries of the dreamscape, but also served as a stark reminder to the guild of the unpredictable and dangerous forces that lurk within the dream realm. The heroes' exceptional performance under such dire circumstances suggests they may play a significant role in the guild's future operations.*
