**Location:** [[Varrock]] → [[Lord Marcus Blackmoor]] Estate  
**Key Figures:** [[Lord Marcus Blackmoor]], [[Brennan]], [[Marta]], [[Gareth and Lily]], [[Willem]], [[Elena]]

## The Crisis

Word has reached [[The Dreamweavers Guild]] of a mysterious ailment plaguing the young son of [[Lord Marcus Blackmoor]] in the city of [[Varrock]]. The boy lies in an unnatural coma, and no healer, wizard, or cleric has been able to wake him. The nobleman's growing desperation and distraction from his duties is causing political and economic instability throughout the city.

## The Heroes' Arrival

**Aargon** - Paladin  
**Ronan** - Ranger *(unaware this is his hometown)*  
**Ryker** - Druid

The guild sends the newly inducted heroes to investigate what appears to be a dreamscape-related crisis. As they approach Lord Blackmoor's estate, they find a household under stress - overworked staff, heightened security, and an atmosphere of barely contained desperation.

*Theme: Something is very wrong here*

**Room 1 - Entrance & Guardian:** 
- The party arrives uninvited at Lord Blackmoor's estate, having heard rumors of the mysterious illness
- **Guardian Challenge:** Convincing the suspicious household staff to grant access to the lord and his son
- **Initial Situation:** The staff are overwhelmed, suspicious of outsiders, and protective of their lord's privacy

**Multiple Approach Options:**

**The Social Approach - Head Steward Brennan:**
- **Challenge:** Brennan is polite but firm - "His lordship sees no one without appointment"
- **Concrete Resolution:** DC 15 Persuasion with advantage if party mentions guild credentials or dream ailments. DC 12 if they offer specific examples of dream-sickness symptoms.
- **What He Reveals:** "Three healers, two wizards, and a priest have all failed. His lordship grows more desperate each day."
- **Exact Success:** Grants 30-minute audience with Lord Blackmoor in his study, provides warning about the lord's emotional state

**The Service Approach - Cook Marta:**
- **The Problem:** Marta needs moonbell mushrooms (grow only in moonlight) to make a broth that might help the boy's appetite
- **Concrete Task:** Mushrooms are in the estate's own moonlight garden, but it's been overrun by 3 dire rats (CR 1/8 each) after the groundskeeper fell ill
- **Alternative:** DC 13 Nature check to identify that silvercap mushrooms from the market district have the same properties (costs 2 gold)
- **Exact Success:** Marta gives party servant uniforms, maps of the house, and warns them "Master Torreck watches everything - avoid the west wing"

**The Protection Approach - Stable Master Gareth:**
- **The Setup:** Two thugs (CR 1/4 each) corner Gareth's daughter Lily demanding "50 gold for protection services Lord Blackmoor ordered"
- **Combat Resolution:** Thugs fight but flee at half health, shouting threats about "paying what's owed"
- **Social Resolution:** DC 14 Investigation reveals the debt document is forged (wrong seal, fresh ink). DC 12 Intimidation makes thugs admit they were "hired to test security"
- **Exact Success:** Grateful Gareth provides stable key (back entrance), reveals "Men have been watching the estate for weeks," and arranges meeting through Lily who works as a scullery maid

**The Bribery Approach - Groundskeeper's Assistant Willem:**
- **The Target:** Willem is owed 3 months wages (15 gold) and his wife is pregnant
- **Concrete Offer:** 20 gold gets full cooperation. 10 gold gets reluctant help. 5 gold gets refused.
- **What's Included:** Willem provides groundskeeper uniform, tells party the boy's window faces the rose garden, and reveals "Strange sounds come from the boy's room at night - like whispers"
- **The Risk:** DC 13 Insight reveals Willem is nervous about something beyond just money

**The Sympathy Approach - Nursemaid Elena:**
- **The Setup:** Elena sits in the servant's hall crying over untouched food prepared for the boy
- **Concrete Trigger:** Any mention of caring for children, healing, or similar experience gets her attention
- **The Test:** She asks the party to demonstrate any healing ability on her arthritic hands (even a cantrip works)
- **What She Reveals:** "The nightmares started exactly 3 weeks ago, the same night those 'debt collectors' first came around. Before that, he was just tired."
- **Exact Success:** Elena smuggles party up the servant stairs and provides 10 minutes alone with the boy before the lord arrives

**Environmental Clues During Approach:**
- **Observation:** Well-maintained grounds but overworked staff
- **Insight:** Some servants seem genuinely worried, others appear to be watching the party carefully
- **Investigation:** Recent visitors include several failed healers, a mysterious "consultant," and increased security presence
- **Perception:** The house feels oppressive, like something is watching from the upper windows

**Failure Consequences:**
- **Turned Away:** Party must find alternative approach or wait until evening to try again
- **Suspicion Raised:** Guards are called, making future attempts more difficult
- **Mixed Success:** Gain access but with limited time or restricted areas

**Success Rewards:**
- **Access Granted:** Meeting with Lord Blackmoor arranged
- **Ally Gained:** Sympathetic staff member provides ongoing information
- **Intelligence Gathered:** Background details about the boy's condition and timeline
- **Reputation:** Word spreads that the party is genuinely trying to help (useful for later sessions)

**Room 1 Conclusion:**
Regardless of approach, the party gains access but also gets their first sense that this household is under pressure from multiple directions. The staff's varied reactions hint at larger tensions - some are protective, others seem scared, and a few appear to be watching the party more carefully than natural curiosity would explain.

**Room 2 - Puzzle & Roleplaying:**
**The Timeline Discrepancy Puzzle**

**Setup:** Now with access to the household, the party can interview key people about the boy's condition. The puzzle emerges as different people give conflicting accounts of when and how the illness began.

**Key Interviews:**

**Dr. Aldwin (Family Physician):**
- **Medical Timeline:** "The boy fell ill approximately 6 weeks ago with what appeared to be a wasting sickness"
- **Symptoms Observed:** Fatigue, loss of appetite, gradual withdrawal from activities
- **Treatments Attempted:** Tonics, bloodletting, herbal remedies - all ineffective
- **Current Assessment:** "I fear this may be beyond conventional medicine"

**Master Cornelius (The Boy's Tutor):**
- **Educational Timeline:** "Young master stopped attending lessons about 4 weeks ago"  
- **Behavioral Changes:** "Before that, he seemed distracted, claimed he couldn't concentrate"
- **Specific Detail:** "He kept saying his dreams were 'too loud' - thought it was just childish complaints"
- **Inconsistency:** Boy was still active and talking 4 weeks ago, contradicting the doctor's 6-week timeline

**Elena (Nursemaid):**
- **Care Timeline:** "The real change was exactly 3 weeks ago - that's when he stopped responding properly"
- **The Critical Night:** "I remember because those debt men had come the night before, yelling and carrying on"
- **Before/After:** "The first night after they came, he seemed scared but was still talking. The second night, he just... never woke up properly"
- **Physical Evidence:** Shows the party the boy's journal - the final entry is chilling: "Had the scary dream again. The same men from yesterday were chasing me but I got away and woke up. Why did I dream about those debt men? Tomorrow I'll tell Papa about—" Entry ends mid-sentence

**Lord Blackmoor:**
- **Parental Timeline:** "My son has been... struggling... for months now"
- **Vague Details:** Cannot provide specific dates, becomes agitated when pressed
- **Contradiction:** Claims multiple failed treatments over "months" but servants say specialists only started coming 3 weeks ago
- **Tells:** Becomes defensive when asked about the debt collectors Elena mentioned

**The Puzzle Resolution:**
**What Players Must Realize:**
1. **Two Different Conditions:** The boy had normal fatigue/distraction for 2-3 weeks, then suddenly entered the coma-like state exactly 3 weeks ago
2. **The Trigger Event:** Something specific happened 3 weeks ago that changed everything
3. **The Cover-Up:** Lord Blackmoor is being deliberately vague to hide the connection to the "debt collectors"
4. **Supernatural Element:** The boy's complaints about "loud dreams" suggest this isn't a medical condition

**Concrete Clues to Discover:**
- **Physical Evidence:** Boy's journal reveals he dreamed about the debt collectors the night they arrived, escaped that dream, then never finished writing the next day
- **Two-Night Pattern:** Debt collectors came first night, boy had scary dream but escaped; second night he fell into the coma
- **Recognition:** Boy specifically recognized the men in his dream as the same debt collectors  
- **Deliberate Attack:** This suggests the supernatural condition was a targeted assault, not random illness
- **Timing:** Boy was going to tell his father something important but never got the chance

**The Resolution (DC 15 Investigation or Insight):**
Players who successfully piece together the timeline realize:
- This isn't a gradual illness - something specific triggered the boy's condition exactly 3 weeks ago
- The trigger coincides with the arrival of the mysterious debt collectors
- Lord Blackmoor knows more than he's admitting about the connection
- The boy's "loud dreams" comment suggests supernatural involvement

**Room 2 Conclusion:**
The party has concrete evidence that this is not a natural illness and that Lord Blackmoor is hiding crucial information about what really happened 3 weeks ago. They're now ready to either confront him directly or investigate the supernatural angle.

**Room 3 - Trick & Setback:**
**The Expulsion and Infiltration**

**The Trick - Different Expulsions Based on Room 1 Approach:**

**If Players Used Brennan's Formal Route:**
**The Confrontation Escalates:**
- During their interview with Lord Blackmoor, players present evidence of the supernatural connection
- Lord Blackmoor becomes increasingly agitated when they mention the debt collectors and the journal
- **Quote:** "You don't understand what you're meddling with! My son's condition is... it's complicated!"
- When pressed about the connection, he explodes: "GET OUT! All of you! I won't have strangers tearing apart my family's private affairs!"
- Brennan reluctantly escorts them out, apologizing: "I'm sorry, his lordship is... not himself lately."

**If Players Used Any Other Route (Sneaking In):**
**Discovery by Security:**
- **Master Torreck** or **Brennan** discovers them during their investigation
- **Torreck's Confrontation:** "I knew something was off about you lot. Trespassing, interrogating servants, spreading panic!"
- **Brennan's Discovery:** "How did you get in here? This is highly irregular - I must ask you to leave immediately!"
- **Evidence Against Them:** Their sneaky approach makes them look suspicious, especially asking about finances and debts
- **Security Response:** Guards are called, they're escorted out as potential threats

**The Setback - Ejection:**
- Party is escorted off the grounds by guards
- Told not to return or they'll be arrested for trespassing
- Any allies from Room 1 seem nervous and won't make eye contact
- Gates are locked, extra guards posted

**The Infiltration Challenge:**
Now the party must use their wits and any connections from Room 1 to get back inside:

**Heightened Security:** Everyone is on higher alert after the expulsion. Getting caught during re-infiltration attempts will potentially lead to guards attacking rather than simply escorting the party away.

**Potential Approaches:**
- **Marta's Route:** Kitchen entrance, but requires completing her mushroom task if not done earlier
- **Willem's Route:** Garden access, but need to deal with dire rats and avoid night patrols  
- **Gareth/Lily's Route:** Stable entrance, but must help resolve their "debt collector" problem first
- **Elena's Route:** She might sneak them in if convinced the boy is in immediate supernatural danger
- **Brennan's Route:** Hardest path - formal reinstatement requires proving their innocence

**Getting to the Boy:**
Once inside, the party must:
- Navigate the house without being caught (Stealth checks)
- Avoid Torreck's patrols (Investigation to learn timing, or Stealth to avoid)
- Reach the boy's room on the second floor
- Deal with locked doors (thieves' tools, magic, or friendly staff assistance)

**The Investigation:**
When they finally reach the boy:
- **Physical Examination:** He appears to be in a deep, unnatural sleep
- **Magical Detection:** Clear supernatural aura around him, dream-magic signatures
- **Observation:** His eyes move rapidly as if experiencing intense dreams
- **Attempted Communication:** Normal means don't work - he's trapped in the dreamscape

**Room 3 Conclusion:**
The party has confirmed the supernatural nature of the attack and identified that the boy is trapped in dreams, but their investigation triggers the nightmare entity's awareness. Strange sounds begin coming from the boy's room, shadows move wrong, and the temperature drops - something knows they're there and doesn't want them interfering.

*Rooms 1-3 provide a complete investigation arc ending with expulsion from the manor. This is an ideal stopping point for shorter sessions, with the party having discovered the supernatural connection and timeline mystery. If continuing in the same session, take a 5-minute break before proceeding to the re-infiltration challenge.*

**Room 4 - Climax & Conflict:**
**Two Paths to the Nightmare Entity**

**Setup:** The party has finally reached the boy and confirmed the supernatural nature of his condition. Now they must decide how to proceed, but either path leads to a dangerous encounter they cannot win.

---

**Path A: The Dreamwalking Attempt**

**The Challenge:** Entering shared dreams without a Sleep Talker is extremely dangerous, but the party might attempt it anyway.

**Getting a Sleep Talker:**
- **Drift Infiltration:** If players are clever, they might try to contact Drift and sneak him in
- **Solo Attempt:** One player with natural dream abilities might risk going alone
- **Makeshift Ritual:** Using guild knowledge to attempt dangerous unguided dream entry

**The Dream Encounter:**
- **Dream Setting:** A twisted version of the Blackmoor estate where hallways stretch endlessly and rooms shift like mazes
- **The Boy's Presence:** They find him running through nightmare corridors, pursued by shadow figures
- **The Nightmare Entity:** A **Lesser Demon** summoned by the Nightshade Cartel as a supernatural debt collector
- **Entity's Appearance:** Shifting shadow with too many grasping hands, glowing red eyes, and whispers in multiple voices - classic demonic features twisted for psychological torment

**Combat in Dreams:**
- **Unstable Environment:** Rooms shift, gravity changes, reality bends to the demon's will
- **Demonic Advantages:** In dreams, the demon has enhanced power and can manipulate the environment freely
- **No Victory Condition:** The demon is bound by contract and too powerful in its domain - party must retreat
- **Information Gained:** "The mortal lords bound me with gold and blood! This child's suffering feeds the contract until payment is made!"
- **Escape:** Party must flee through nightmare logic, possibly saving the boy temporarily but can't break the binding

---

**Path B: Provoking Physical Manifestation**

**The Trigger:** Instead of entering dreams, the party investigates the supernatural aura around the boy.

**Magical Investigation:**
- **Detect Magic:** Reveals powerful enchantment and extraplanar (Abyssal) presence
- **Dispel Attempts:** Trying to break the spell doesn't work but disrupts the demon's feeding
- **Direct Interference:** Healing magic, blessing, or protective wards around the boy

**The Demon's Response:**
- **Forced Manifestation:** The lesser demon partially materializes in the physical world, furious at the interference
- **Physical Form:** Semi-corporeal shadow creature with demonic features - claws, burning eyes, sulfurous smoke
- **Contract Driven:** "This child belongs to me! The Nightshade compact must be fulfilled! His father's debt will be paid in suffering!"

**Physical Combat:**
- **Goal:** Not to destroy (impossible at their level) but to disrupt the contract and force retreat
- **Demonic Tactics:** Fear effects, shadow attacks, attempts to drag party into nightmares
- **Weakness:** Less powerful in physical realm, vulnerable to radiant damage and holy magic
- **Contract Limitations:** Cannot actually kill the boy - only torment him until "payment" terms are met
- **Information Gained:** "Lord Blackmoor summoned us through intermediaries! The Nightshade Cartel holds our binding contracts!"
- **Resolution:** Demon retreats but warns "The contract remains! Others will come if I fail!"

---

**Room 4 Resolution - Both Paths:**

**What Players Learn:**
- **Cartel Connection:** References to "Nightshade" confirm criminal organization involvement
- **Demonic Contract:** This isn't random - someone specifically hired this entity
- **Ongoing Threat:** The boy remains trapped, but the entity is now aware of their interference
- **Power Level:** They're outmatched and need better preparation/backup

**Immediate Consequences:**
- **Boy's Status:** Temporarily stabilized but still trapped
- **Entity's Awareness:** Now knows the party is a threat and will be more aggressive
- **Time Pressure:** Situation is escalating, they need to act soon
- **Next Steps:** Clear they need more information about the cartel and better preparation

**Session 1 Cliffhanger:**
Whether they fought in dreams or reality, the party has confirmed a demonic contract with the Nightshade Cartel is keeping the boy trapped. The entity retreats with threats about "the mortals who hired me," and the boy remains unconscious. They've gained crucial intelligence but are clearly in over their heads - time to report back to the guild and prepare for a more serious intervention.

## Encounter Stat Blocks

### 1. Dire Rats (Easy but Fun)
**3 Dire Rats** - *Small beast, unaligned*
- **AC** 11, **HP** 7 (2d4+2), **Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **STR** 7, **DEX** 12, **CON** 14, **INT** 2, **WIS** 10, **CHA** 4
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10

**Tactics:** Attack in pack, try to surround one character. Flee when reduced to 2 HP.

### 2. Debt Collector Thugs (Easy but Fun)
**2 Thugs** - *Medium humanoid, chaotic evil*
- **AC** 12 (Leather Armor), **HP** 32 (5d8+10), **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 15, **DEX** 11, **CON** 14, **INT** 10, **WIS** 10, **CHA** 11
- **Skills** Intimidation +2
- **Senses** passive Perception 10
- **Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP each)

**Multiattack.** The thug makes two melee attacks.
**Mace.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
**Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.

**Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls when ally within 5 feet of target.
**Tactics:** Focus fire on one target, flee at half health shouting threats. Not looking for a real fight.

### 3. Blackmoor Estate Guards (Potentially Problematic)
**4 Guards** - *Medium humanoid, lawful neutral*
- **AC** 16 (Chain shirt, shield), **HP** 22 (4d8+4), **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 14, **DEX** 12, **CON** 12, **INT** 10, **WIS** 12, **CHA** 10
- **Skills** Perception +4
- **Senses** passive Perception 14
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each, but dangerous in groups)

**Spear.** *Melee/Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage if used with two hands.

**Formation Fighting.** When adjacent to another guard, +2 AC against opportunity attacks.
**Alarm.** Can use action to blow horn, alerting entire estate (reinforcements arrive in 3 rounds).
**Tactics:** Fight defensively, try to surround intruders, call for backup immediately. Will accept surrender.

### 4. Lesser Demon of Torment (Really Tough - Flee Encounter)
**Lesser Demon** - *Medium fiend (demon), chaotic evil*
- **AC** 15 (Natural Armor), **HP** 85 (10d8+40), **Speed** 30 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)
- **STR** 16, **DEX** 14, **CON** 18, **INT** 12, **WIS** 13, **CHA** 16
- **Saving Throws** Wis +4, Cha +6
- **Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire; Bludg/Pier/Slash from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Vulnerabilities** Radiant
- **Damage Immunities** Poison
- **Condition Immunities** Poisoned
- **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages** Abyssal, Common
- **Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP) - **Not meant to be defeated!**

**Multiattack.** The demon makes two claw attacks.
**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage.
**Nightmare Grasp** *(Recharge 5-6).* Target within 30 ft. must make DC 14 Wisdom save or be pulled into a shared nightmare (incapacitated, takes 2d8 psychic damage per turn until freed by ally's action).
**Fear Aura.** Each creature within 30 feet must make DC 14 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute (repeat save each turn).

**Contract Bound.** Cannot kill the boy. Takes 10 damage if it moves more than 60 feet from the boy's room.
**Semi-Corporeal.** In physical form, reduce all stats by 25% but gains vulnerability to radiant damage.
**Retreat Condition.** Flees to dreamscape when reduced to 30 HP or less, or when faced with significant radiant damage.

## GM Notes

**Encounter Scaling:**
- **Rats/Thugs:** Warm-up encounters, should be dispatched quickly
- **Guards:** Real threat if party gets careless - designed to encourage stealth
- **Demon:** Major story beat - party should realize they need to retreat and find another solution

**Session Outcomes:**
- **Critical Information:** Boy is trapped by demonic contract with Nightshade Cartel
- **Personal Stakes:** Ronan recognizes this as his hometown (connection to parents' death unrevealed)
- **Tactical Situation:** Party has disrupted the supernatural attack but not ended it
- **Next Steps:** Need to investigate cartel connections and find way to break the contract

**Key NPCs Established:**
- **Lord Marcus Blackmoor:** Desperate father hiding guilty secrets
- **Elena:** Sympathetic nursemaid, potential ongoing ally
- **Brennan:** Professional steward caught between loyalty and duty
- **Marta, Willem, Gareth & Lily:** Household staff with varying degrees of trust

## Potential Rewards

**Lord Blackmoor's Payment:**
- 200 gold upon agreeing to help + 500 gold if they stabilize his son
- 2-3 healing potions from his personal stock
- Permanent welcome to the manor for future rest

**Grateful Staff Rewards** *(Given based on which NPCs the party helped/befriended):*

**Marta's Moonbell Mushroom Broth:** Special preserved meal that provides the benefits of a long rest when consumed (1 use). *"Made with the mushrooms you helped me gather - it'll restore you like a full night's peaceful sleep."*

**Brennan's Letter of Recommendation:** Grants advantage on social interactions with other noble households' stewards. *"A sealed letter of introduction that will open doors in the noble quarter."*

**Elena's Lullaby Pendant:** Small pendant that gives +1 to saves against fear effects. Made from the boy's baby blanket and enchanted with nursemaid's love. *"When worn, the bearer feels the comfort of being safely held in loving arms, like a child protected by their devoted caretaker."*

**A Father's Handshake** *(From Gareth & Lily):* If you help relieve them of the harassment, Gareth teaches you his firm, calming handshake. Grants advantage on Persuasion or Intimidation checks against young men, but only after you've first shaken their hand. *"A working man's handshake that shows you mean business without saying a word."*
