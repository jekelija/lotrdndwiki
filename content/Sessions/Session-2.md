📜 Summary

The party meets with Ebrulf, the head hobbit of the village they saved. He asks for their help in locating his nephew, Reginard, who left to go adventuring long ago and never returned, despite promising to come home.

During their conversation, Ebrulf recounts stories passed down from his great-grandfather, who lived in the Shire during Saruman’s occupation. While he recalls no mention of a crown, he does remember that Saruman recruited hobbits as agents, sending them east to recover gems of great power.

Ebrulf’s ancestor was part of the resistance and participated in raids against these agents. As a token of trust, Ebrulf gifts the party a family heirloom:

💎 Quest Item

Pure White Gem
Stolen from Saruman’s agents generations ago and passed down in secret

As the discussion concludes, Feldrog and Thalan join the party, bolstering their ranks.

🗺️ Journey Continues

The party resumes their journey toward Isengard, passing through the long-abandoned river town of Tharbad. While exploring nearby barrows, they discover a sealed stone door.

🧩 Puzzle & Combat

The party solves the door’s puzzle.

Several Barrow-Wraiths emerge from the tomb.

The wraiths are defeated after a hard-fought battle.

🔚 Session End

With the barrows cleared, the party prepares to explore the tomb’s interior.
